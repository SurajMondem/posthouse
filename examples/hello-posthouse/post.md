# Hello, posthouse

You wrote a great post. Most people will scroll right past it, but they will stop for a video. Making that video by
hand takes an afternoon; posthouse makes it from the post.

## How it works

1. **Script.** It reads your post and writes a short script: one idea per scene, and every number comes straight
   from your words. You approve the plan before anything is made.
2. **Voice.** A voice reads the script. Use ElevenLabs (including a clone of your own voice), a free voice that runs
   on your own machine, or no voice at all for a silent, captioned video.
3. **Timing.** Every word gets a timestamp, so the picture moves at the exact moment the voice says it.
4. **Stage.** There are no slides. One camera glides across a single stage, carrying each idea into the next, with
   soft sounds on every move.

Point it at a post and get a video back. posthouse is free and open source.
